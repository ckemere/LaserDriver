**StimHAT** (`LaserHAT/StimHAT/`, branch `worktree-stim-hat` off `battery-pack`, a git worktree at
`.claude/worktrees/stim-hat`): one 65 x 57 mm HAT + 10 x 7.5 mm south-east tab (vertical electrode header J101
only; PS1 stands on top in the band north of the right-angle BNC bodies - the vertical-BNC option was dropped to free
it, 2026-10-29 late - and J102, the battery socket, shares PS1's space with its pads 2.55 mm south of PS1's pins, one
of the two fitted; J10 is a vertical OLED socket at the top strip's west end, the module standing up along the header;
nothing hangs below the HAT) carrying the Rev 2 core (MSPM0G3507, CH340N,
USB-C path, EEPROM, BNCs, FIRE, joystick) and the kbest stimulator isolated by ISO7741F + ISO1640, powered by the A0515S
(PS1, hand-fitted, standing north of the BNCs) or the battery pack (J102 B4B-PH-K-S).  Full record of the decisions and
rejected options in `LaserHAT/claude_memory/stim-hat-plan.md`; build details and checks in `StimHAT/README.md`.

Key facts not obvious from the code:
- The user wants the **bonnet (Adafruit 4567) kept** and a 0.91 in 128 x 32 module as the fallback (J10, unpopulated);
  no 128 x 64 panel (Pi software change).  Latency is sacred (no photoMOS SHORT switch); readback of the DAC matters.
- Pi side must use **GPIO16 for the MCU reset** on this board (`LASERHAT_RESET_PIN=16`); firmware adds BUTTON6 (PA3,
  joystick right); joystick is mounted at 45 degrees.
- pcb_sync-copied footprints share UUIDs -> run `tools/fresh_uuids.py` or KiCad's DRC pairs the wrong parts.
- Hand-fit: J1, J6, J7, J101, J102, PS1 (DNP in the BOM; never with the pack at the same time), J10 optional; MH4 nylon.
- **Routed and audited 2026-10-29** (commits d7c196f, 6f0c606): Freerouting alone never finishes (2–9 leftovers per
  run, a different set each time, stops by itself after ~300 passes); `tools/fanout.py` (locked dog-bone stubs +
  staggered vias on every U7/U101 pin, GND thermal vias in U7's pad) and `tools/preroute.py` (SDA_H, the tab's five
  isolated nets through the corridor west of MH4, the +5V/GND passage) get it to 2–3, the rest was finished by hand
  with `tools/fixups.py` (track / via / move-via / drop-seg) using matplotlib renders of the copper (scratch
  `plot_area.py` / `plot_layer.py`); re-running a full route after a placement change costs ~10 min and reshuffles the
  leftovers, so prefer hand fixes on the current board.  **KiCad 9 silently ignores a .kicad_dru rule that has a
  `(comment ...)` entry** — the barrier rule had never fired until the comments were removed; always prove the rule
  file live with a deliberate 25 mm test.  `pcbnew` segfaults on `FOOTPRINT.Remove(graphic)` in this build: edit the
  .kicad_pcb text instead (`fixups.py silk-strip`, `tools/silk_dedupe.py`).  The 4 remaining DRC warnings
  (lib_footprint_mismatch on J6, J7, J101, PS1) are the stripped edge-overhanging silk and are expected.  No copper
  pours by design.  Two rounds of independent audits (schematic, layout) were run and their MAJORs fixed; see
  `StimHAT/README.md` (commits 6f0c606, 4056d5c, d128fba, b5c1049, 5207846, daeabbd; board audits #4 and #5 (after PS1's move) = no blocker, #5's only major is the README's USB back-powering claim).  **Round 6 (2026-10-29 night): placement pushed unrouted on purpose - the user routes the 23 open connections by hand** (their request); the PS1 footprint orientation was checked against the Mornsun drawing (third-angle: pins 0.9 mm from the back face, pin 1 left in the marked-face view = pin 1 west, marked face south) and kept, pending the user's check on a physical module.  **4 layers since 2026-10-29 night** (user's call): In1 = GND / GND_ISO planes generated by place.py, In2 = power, routed by the user; all tracks/vias ripped on request (the user routes), DRC 0/0 with 300 open connections at commit time.  **MCU = MSPM0G1507SRHBR since 2026-10-29 night** (the G3507 minus CAN-FD, pin-compatible in RHB, firmware unaffected: it uses TIMA0, TIMG0/6/7/8/12, UART0/1, I2C0, DAC0, ADC1 only); LCSC C22406180 (user-supplied; C33740407 is the G1107 - no DAC / OPA / COMP - not it).  The bonnet (Adafruit 4567 = 128x64 1.3" OLED bonnet, EagleCAD on GitHub) is drawn on layer User.1 "Bonnet" by place.py.  **Barrier rules reworked (user found the router's 2.5 mm halos unusable):** no clearance rule any more; place.py makes two no-keepout rule areas `hat_region` / `isolated_region` whose gap is exactly the 2.5 mm keep-out strips, and the .kicad_dru disallows copper of the other side inside each region (`A.intersectsArea`, pads need `A.Type != 'Pad' || A.Pad_Type != 'NPTH, mechanical'` or vias/tracks are skipped); the distance is then geometric - keep the strips 2.5 wide and the regions 2.5 apart at every corner when editing the floorplan.  **The isolated supply left the HAT (2026-10-29 night, user's decision):** PS1 (A0515S), C104, L101/L102 + C105/C106 and the Q101/Q102 capacitance multipliers are gone (112 parts now); J102 (JST-PH in the pocket, pads +/-V_STIM, GND_ISO x2 at y 40.1) is the only +/-V input - the battery pack by cable, or a **DC-DC pod** (those parts on a ~30 x 6.5 mm board, still to be designed; README section "The DC-DC pod") plugged into J102's holes and fed by J103 (1x2 2.54 header, 5V/GND, DNP, at (19/21.54, 40.1) on the HAT side of the band); the pod lies over the band with its own gap above the HAT's strip at x 34-36.5.  Nets +V_RAW/-V_RAW/+V_F/-V_F/QB_P/QB_N no longer exist (Rails class = +/-V_STIM).  **2026-10-29 afternoon: the user re-placed and routed the isolated block by hand (commits 03d3c56, 912a3a7)** - never run place.py without first syncing its table from the board (reshape.py did: 50 parts); tab removed, BNCs at x 24.92 / 40.92, strips/regions/planes reshaped; In2 = +5V / MSPM0_3V3 (HAT) and +5V_ISO / +V_STIM (isolated) islands; F/B GND pours; isolated power routed by me (powerfan vias + tools/maze_nets.py + tools/stitch.py).  The HAT side is the user's to route.  **MCU pin map re-optimised 2026-10-29** (table in StimHAT/README.md; EN PA23, CATH PA22, FAULT PA26, SDA_H/SCL_H PA24/25 as ADC0 ch3/2, STIM_IN PA21, STIM_OUT PA17, LED PA5, buttons PA6/7/12/13/14/16; PA3/PA4 spare); Firmware/board.h + mcu.h hold it as an UNCOMMITTED draft, the .c files are not yet adapted.  The DC-DC pod project is LaserHAT/DCDCPod (rev A, routed, DRC 0/0, perpendicular with RA headers).  More gotchas: `.kicad_dru` conditions select a footprint's
  pads with `A.memberOfFootprint('J102')` (`A.Reference` never matches); `pcb_sync.py --keep-tracks` does not swap a
  changed footprint id — `fixups.py swap-fp` does (keeps position / side / nets / path); a module whose body must lie
  on the other side of its pin row needs a mirrored footprint (`DCDC_SIP6_A0515S_BodyN`, generated by
  `make_footprints.py`), not a rotation (that reverses the pin order).
  **2026-10-29 evening:** the USB-C power / switch / CH340 corner was regrouped and hand-routed by me (README "USB-C corner"; the route script lives only in the job tmp dir): VBUS -> Q5 -> Q3 -> +5V as one column east of J10, D+/D- down x 10.8 / 12.1 into U6, PWR_EN and the VBUS_SW sense on In2; the UART corridor U7.14/15 -> U6.7/6 is left free for the user.  DRC 0/0, 96 opens (all the user's: 3V3 rails, I2C, NRST, UART, buttons, U7's thermal pad).
  **2026-10-29 night, pin change at the user's request:** PI_TRIGGER = Pi GPIO10 (J1.19) -> U7 pin 8 (PA4); RELEASE_H = pin 31 (PA27); pin 19 (PA15, DAC0_OUT) and pin 7 (PA3) spare; J1.37 (GPIO26) free.  GPIO10 was chosen because GPIO4/17/22/23/27/5/6 belong to the OLED bonnet and GPIO7/8 pull up at boot (false rising edge); SPI0 must stay disabled on the Pi.  The Pi software still defaults to GPIO26 (Rev 2 HAT): StimHAT needs LASERHAT_TRIGGER_PIN=10 / `laserhat-brokerd --gpio-pin 10` and power_cycle.py's QUIET_PINS - not changed yet.  Firmware/board.h + mcu.h drafts updated (BOARD_ESTIM_RELEASE_PIN now its own PA27 define; module.c / gpio.c still assume RELEASE on the DAC pin).
  **Later the same night:** U7's east column re-ordered to U101's pin order (user's request): 31 SCL_H PA27 (ADC0 ch0), 30 SDA_H PA26 (ch1), 29 RELEASE_H PA25, 28 EN_H PA24 (laser PWM_A = TIMA0_CCP3N or TIMA1_CCP1), 27 CATH_H PA23 (TIMA0_CCP3), 26 FAULT_H PA22 (GPIO IRQ only), 25 STIM_IN PA21.  Firmware drafts follow it; mcu.h still lacks the PA24 PWM function code.
  **Header + buttons (2026-10-29, late):** PI_TRIGGER = Pi GPIO18 (J1.12), MCU_PWR_EN = GPIO10 (J1.19), NRST = GPIO9 (J1.21); U7: 9 spare, 10 LED_MCU PA6, 11 BUTTON4 (up) PA7, 16 BUTTON1 (left) PA12 (1/4 swapped later for the user's routing), 17 BUTTON6 (right) PA13, 18 BUTTON2 (centre) PA14, 19 BUTTON3 (down) PA15, 20 BUTTON5 (FIRE) PA16 - a 45-degree fan to SW7 in pad order (pads 4, 6 / 3, 2, 1).  SW7 sits at 135 deg: ALPS contacts A1/B4/C3/D6 = push S/W/E/N = pads 1 down, 4 left, 3 right, 6 up (assumed 'push toward the arrow closes that contact' - verify on hardware).  Pi env for StimHAT: LASERHAT_NRST_PIN=9 LASERHAT_POWER_PIN=10 LASERHAT_TRIGGER_PIN=18, brokerd --gpio-pin 18.

**Why:** the module + HAT + pack chain had become more complex and costlier than two dedicated HATs; see the plan note.
**How to apply:** treat StimHAT as a generated project (edit `sch_parts.py` / `gen_schematic.py` / `tools/place.py`, never
the .kicad files by hand); the laser HAT would reuse the same core block.
